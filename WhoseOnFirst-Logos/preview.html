WHOSE ON FIRST

Logo Concept Exploration — March 2026

Concept 1 — Rotation

Team Cycle

Three cycling arrows around a person icon — emphasizes the rotation aspect of on-call. The gold "#1" badge calls out who's up. Blue-to-purple gradient feels modern and tech-forward.

Concept 2 — Shield

Guardian

Shield silhouette with a person at center, surrounded by subtle clock markers. "#1" anchors the bottom. Dark slate tones with cyan accent — protective, reliable, professional.

Concept 3 — Spotlight

Who's Up

One person highlighted among three — the "on first" person is solid white while teammates fade back. Gold crown/pointer above. Green palette says "active/go." App-icon friendly rounded square.

Concept 4 — Typographic

Minimal

Bold geometric "W" with a subtle person (gold dot) at the center peak. The subscript "1" completes the wordmark. Indigo-to-violet gradient. Ultra-clean, works at any size.
