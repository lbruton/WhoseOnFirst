Round 2 — Spotlight Concept Variants

Building on the "who's up" highlighted-person direction from Concept 3

Original Concept 3

Variant A — Neon Dark

Cyberpunk / Dev Tool

Dark indigo background with cyan-to-purple neon outlines. The active person glows while teammates are dim gray. Diamond indicator above, numbered badge at bottom-right. Feels like a dev tool dashboard.

Variant B — Clean Flat

Corporate / Universal

Classic blue with simplified flat silhouettes. The center person is solid white and slightly larger — teammates are ghost-faded. Gold "#1" circle badge above. Clean, professional, works everywhere. Most versatile of the bunch.

Variant C — Rotation Ring

Dynamic / Cycle

Keeps the green from the original but adds a gold dashed orbit ring to emphasize rotation. Three team dots sit on the ring path, center person is highlighted inside. "1ST" badge in gold. Communicates movement and sequence.

Variant D — Podium

Playful / Gamified

Leans into the "first" part of the name with a podium/winners-stand metaphor. Gold first-place block, star above. Lighthearted and fun — good if the app has a playful personality. Sky blue palette.
